# Audio credits

> Generated files under `sfx/gen/` and `music/stems/` are **not stored in the
> repo**. Run `python tools/gen_sfx.py` and `python tools/gen_music.py` to
> produce them (deterministic, ~10 s, needs numpy and scipy).

Plasma Pong used FMOD and licensed cinematic tracks (ES Posthumus *Pompeii*,
Harry Gregson-Williams). Those are not in this repo.

Files here are public domain (CC0). Credit is not required. We list authors anyway.

## Music

- `music/match.ogg` — *Hope (Orchestral battle music)* by MintoDog.
  https://opengameart.org/content/hopeorchestral-battle-music
  CC0. The "hope" soundtrack (menu default).
- `music/stems/synth_pad.wav`, `synth_pulse.wav`, `synth_drive.wav` — the
  "synth" soundtrack (match default). Original, generated by
  `tools/gen_music.py` (numpy/scipy, seeded): 8 bars at 112 BPM in A minor
  (Am F C G), 756000 frames each, seamless, sample-locked by the
  AudioManager. Layer A pad (detuned saws), B pulse (filtered 16th-note bass),
  C drive (kick / hat / snare). CC0. Regenerate with
  `python tools/gen_music.py`.

## Generated sound effects (`sfx/gen/`)

Original, synthesised for this project by `tools/gen_sfx.py` (numpy/scipy,
deterministic seed). Public domain, CC0. Regenerate with
`python tools/gen_sfx.py`.

- Impacts: `paddle_hit_1..3`, `wall_hit_1..3`, `brick_hit_1..2`,
  `brick_shatter`, `parry`
- Blasts: `blast`, `blast_ready`, `super`, `stun`, `stun_bolt_fire`
- Loops (seamless, `smpl` loop chunk): `suck_loop`, `stream_loop`,
  `hydro_rush_loop`, `drone_calm_loop`, `drone_turbulent_loop`,
  `heartbeat_loop`
- Stingers (stereo): `goal_p1`, `goal_p2`, `set_won`, `match_won`,
  `match_lost`, `stage_intro`, `stage_complete`
- Events: `rally_tier_1..4`, `overdrive_riser`, `lock_enter`,
  `powerup_spawn`, `powerup_collect`, `powerup_expire`, `multiball_split`,
  `hazard_vortex`
- UI: `ui_navigate`, `ui_confirm`, `ui_back`, `menu_open`, `pause`,
  `resume`, `countdown_tick`
- Signature moments (phase 2): `blast_charge_loop` (rise + looping hold
  region), `blast_charge_release_1..3`, `suck_capture`, `orbit_loop`
  (seamless), `slingshot_release`, `goal_shatter` (stereo),
  `brick_shard_tinkle`, `serve_beat_1..3`, `serve_warning`, `lock_pulse`,
  `perfect_star`, `wall_ripple`

Still in use from the CC0 sets above: `music/match.ogg` (music), `sfx/pep.mp3`
(layered under the generated paddle hits), `sfx/sting.ogg` (callout stings),
`sfx/goal.ogg` (smash-goal layer). `hit_wall.ogg`, `blast.ogg`, `parry.ogg`
and `super.ogg` are kept in the repo and in the AudioManager library as
alternates but are not triggered by default.

Bus layout: `default_bus_layout.tres` (Master > Music / SFX (Impact, Blast,
World) / Drone / UI). Approximate mix targets: paddle hits -10 dBFS, goal
stingers -6, music -10, UI -16, drone -24.
